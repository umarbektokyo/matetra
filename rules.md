Standard Rules:
Don’t cheat.
At the beginning of the game and after every turn, players must have 6 cards. (refill if not)
A player at any point in time will either be attacking or defending.
On their turn, the player will be able to add numbers to their set, and defend those numbers. On other’s, the player can attack the numbers in an effort to either remove the playing user’s number or decrease it.
Defending entails that a player can add numbers to their set and use theorems and functions on the numbers already in their set.
Attacking entails that a player can NOT add numbers to their own set and they may only use theorems and functions on the numbers in the set of the player currently defending.

Variable Meanings:
d → number must be rolled on a dice
a → the “main number”, if attacking, this is where the attacked number goes.
b,c,… → You can only use your own numbers from the set.
u → call umarbek and ask to generate a random number.]
